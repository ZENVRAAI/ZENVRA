ZENVRA AI Trade Tracker - Privacy Policy

Last updated: REPLACE_WITH_DATE

ZENVRA AI Trade Tracker ("the app") is a market analysis and trade journaling tool. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no developer-operated server.

Data stored on your device

Settings and your trade journal are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it (for example, to an Excel file). Clearing the app's storage deletes it.

Third-party services

To show market prices, the app makes requests directly from your device to public data services, including Binance public market data (binance.com) and public gold price feeds (gold-api.com). These services receive standard connection information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or control that information. The app also loads fonts from Google Fonts and a spreadsheet library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.

No financial advice

The app is an analysis tool. Signals, levels, and summaries are informational and are not financial, investment, or trading advice. The app does not execute trades, hold funds, or connect to your exchange account. Trading leveraged derivatives carries a high risk of loss.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
